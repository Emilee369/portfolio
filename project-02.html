THE PROBLEM

Helping people discover the clinic and stay connected.

Juvenile Caduceus Clinic supports underserved youth through healthcare and community initiatives. Our team worked with JCC to create a website that communicated its mission, organized its information, and helped UC Davis students explore ways to get involved.

For the Alumni page, the goal was to give former members a place to see alumni updates and maintain their connection with the clinic.

TEAM CONTEXT

A website bringing the clinic’s mission, history, committees, partnerships, and alumni information together.

MY OWNERSHIP

The Alumni page: organizing its content and designing an experience for the community beyond current membership.

RESEARCH & DIRECTION

Starting with the client’s priorities.

We clarified JCC’s audience, content needs, and priorities before designing. The brief emphasized simple navigation, organized committee information, and a clear presentation of the clinic’s history and work.

We also reviewed MinuteClinic, Winters Healthcare, and San Francisco Free Clinic to explore how other organizations presented their missions, resources, and contact information.

UI & FINAL EXPERIENCE

A dedicated page within a shared visual system.

The Alumni page sits within the team’s shared design system, which established colors, typography, and interface styles across the website. This kept the page visually connected to JCC’s broader experience.

One visual language.

The system paired DM Sans for display text with Khula for body text. Purple and blue appear in the Alumni hero and its shortcuts, connecting my page to the rest of the clinic’s website.

OUTCOME

Completed as part of JCC’s website project.

Our team completed the JCC website project and presented the designs at #Include Demo Day. I contributed the Alumni page, creating a dedicated space for alumni updates, connection opportunities, and the clinic newsletter.

I’m grateful for the opportunity to work with our design teammates and leads, collaborate with the technical team, and create something for a real client.
